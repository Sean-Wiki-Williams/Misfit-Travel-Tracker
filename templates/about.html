Dad's Flight Tracker

Know where a traveling family member is, what flight they're on, and when they'll be home.

What it does

  • Reads flights from your calendar's iCal feed, or lets you add flights by hand.
  • Shows a daily schedule with departure and arrival times, gates, aircraft and status.
  • Displays a live map with the aircraft's position while the flight is in the air.
  • Counts down to when your traveler will be home.

Share with family

Signed-in users can publish a private, view-only link to their schedule. Anyone with the link can follow along without creating an account, and the owner can turn the link off or replace it at any time.
